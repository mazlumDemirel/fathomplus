FAQ

Frequently asked questions

General
What exactly is Fathom Pulse?+

An Android app that turns a Postman collection into a real load test and a glanceable health dashboard. Import a collection, run a test at whatever intensity makes sense, and optionally pin it so you can check its status - or get a notification the moment it starts failing - without opening the app again.

Do I need a Postman account, or Postman installed?+

No. Fathom reads the standard Postman v2.1 collection/environment JSON format - the same file you'd get from Postman's own "Export" button - but it doesn't talk to Postman's servers or require any Postman account.

Does this replace Postman?+

No - Fathom isn't for building or editing requests in detail, and it doesn't try to be. It's a focused tool for one job: pulse-checking APIs you already have a collection for, especially in a load-testing/monitoring context, from a phone rather than a laptop.

Load testing
How does Fathom decide how much load my phone can generate?+

Before ramping up, it checks your device's CPU core count, available RAM, and network type, and computes a concurrency ceiling from that. Light, Medium, and Heavy are percentages of that ceiling (15%, 40%, 100%), so a preset never asks for more than your specific phone can actually generate.

A short calibration burst also runs first - if the target is already failing or very slow at low concurrency, Fathom stops before ramping up rather than pushing a target that's clearly already in trouble.

What if I'm on cellular data, not Wi-Fi?+

The Heavy preset is automatically capped on cellular, to avoid carrier throttling making a result look worse than the target actually performed.

Does it run my collection's pre-request and test scripts?+

Yes (Pro) - a real embedded JavaScript engine executes your collection's actual, unmodified script source, including HMAC signing helpers and pm.environment.set(...)/pm.environment.get(...). A token a "Login" request's test script captures is available to every later request in that same run, in collection order. Assertion helpers (pm.test, pm.expect) are accepted but treated as no-ops - Fathom is a load tool, not a correctness/assertion tool.

What's the difference between the business view and technical details?+

Every result opens on a plain green/amber/red verdict with a one-sentence reason - meant to be read in two seconds. "Show technical details" (Plus) expands into latency percentiles, throughput/latency-over-time charts, and a per-endpoint breakdown of which request is the weakest link.

Free vs Plus vs Pro
What do I actually get for free?+

Unlimited imports, Light and Medium load presets, the business-friendly verdict, the last 3 runs kept per collection, and 1 pinned dashboard flow - genuinely usable on its own for a quick pulse-check.

What does Plus unlock?+

The Heavy preset and Advanced mode (your own concurrency/duration/ramp-up), full technical details and charts, and 10 kept runs per collection - everything you need for an occasional, real load test. See the full comparison.

What does Pro add on top of Plus?+

Pre-request/test script execution, periodic auto-check with failure notifications and the home-screen widget, up to 10 pinned dashboard flows, and .zip bundle import - the tier for running a flow unattended rather than testing it once. See the full comparison.

How do I manage or cancel my Plus/Pro subscription?+

Both are billed and managed entirely through Google Play, like any other Play subscription - open the Play Store app, go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, and find Fathom Pulse there to change or cancel it. Fathom itself never sees or stores your payment details.

Privacy & data
Where does my collection/environment data live?+

On your device, in local storage - Fathom has no backend of its own and no account system. The only network traffic it ever makes on your behalf is the actual HTTP requests your collection defines, sent straight to whatever URL you configured, plus Google Play Billing and AdMob's own traffic. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Am I allowed to load-test any API I want?+

Only ones you own or are otherwise authorized to test - see the Terms of Service. Fathom is a legitimate testing tool, not a way to attack a service you don't have permission to load.
