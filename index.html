Everything a Postman collection needs to prove an API is healthy

Fathom Pulse turns a collection you already have into a real, device-aware load test - then keeps watching.

Import however you already have it

Paste the JSON, pick a file, share it straight from Drive/Slack/Mail, or drop in a .zip with the collection and its environment together.

A load test that knows your phone's limits

Fathom checks your device's CPU, RAM, and network before picking a safe concurrency - so a "Heavy" run never just crashes your phone instead of testing the target.

Real pre-request & test scripts

An embedded JavaScript engine runs your collection's actual scripts - HMAC signing, and chaining a login token into the next request, exactly like Postman does.

A dashboard you can actually glance at

Pin the flows you care about and see them green or red at a glance - even without unlocking your phone, via the home-screen widget.

It keeps watching after you close the app

Turn on auto-check and Fathom quietly re-runs a pinned flow on your own schedule, and taps you on the shoulder the moment one starts failing.

Your data never leaves your device

No account, no backend, no analytics on what you test. Collections, environments, and results all live in local storage, plain and simple.
